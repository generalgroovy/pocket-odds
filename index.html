What are my chances?

Stones stay out of the bag after a draw. The next chance changes with what remains. A strong choice can still miss; a lucky draw does not prove the choice was best.

“Best possible chance” assumes you choose the best bag after each outcome. It is an exact calculation for these tiny bags, not a guarantee.

How to play

Collect the pictured set before your draws run out. Select a bag, then Draw. Each bag shows every stone still inside: B is blue, A is amber, P is plum. You cannot choose which stone comes out.

Undo restores the draw, its bag choice and random state. Retry uses the same draw order; New draw order changes it. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Four challenges, no wagers and no timer.
